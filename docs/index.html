ChronoSwan Chartbook

Cross-asset option-implied stress and event-conditioned PCA.

ChronoSwan is a research work in progress on point-in-time detection and attribution of market stress. The current branch tests whether futures-options implied volatility, skew, and PCA concentration identify stress regimes early enough to improve a simple S&P 500 exposure rule.

Raw Bloomberg, Nasdaq Data Link, Finaeon, parquet caches, and executed notebooks are kept local. This public page reports methodology and aggregate research results only.

Page 0

Research Question

Question

Can a cross-asset options panel filter out ordinary market noise and identify the important impulses that precede or accompany large equity drawdowns?

Contribution

The pipeline combines cross-asset option-implied stress, event-rate tests, PCA concentration, a strict chronological holdout, and an intraday ES impulse attribution case study.

Page 8

Conclusion And Next Research Work

Conclusion

Cross-asset option-implied data is a serious stress-regime layer. It is strongest for measurement, event-rate lift, and drawdown control; it is not yet a standalone alpha or rare-event oracle.

Next work

Freeze strategy thresholds, add formal hedge-instrument tests, compare PCA against DCC and spillover benchmarks, and add timestamp-valid macro/news synthesis only after the structured market-data signal is locked.
